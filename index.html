FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer on-site support as well as remote support?

Yes. Most issues are resolved quickly through remote support, and our engineers visit your premises whenever on-site work is needed.

What does a managed IT services contract include?

Typically proactive monitoring, helpdesk support, patching and updates, backup checks, security management and regular reviews — tailored to the size and needs of your business.

Can you supply hardware and software licences?

Yes. We source, configure and install laptops, servers, networking equipment, phones, CCTV and software licences, so you have one partner from purchase to support.

Can you work with our existing IT team or provider?

Absolutely. We can fully manage your IT or complement your in-house team with specialist skills, extra capacity or after-hours cover.
