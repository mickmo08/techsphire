FAQ

Common questions

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A focused MVP often takes 8–12 weeks, while larger platforms are delivered in phases. After our discovery call we'll give you a clear timeline and estimate.

Do we own the code you write?

Yes. All source code, designs and documentation produced for your project belong to you.

Can you work with our existing team?

Absolutely. We regularly embed alongside in-house teams, adopting your tools and processes or helping improve them.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer flexible support and maintenance plans, including monitoring, security updates and new feature development.
