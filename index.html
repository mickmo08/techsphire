Frequently asked questions

What size of businesses do you work with?

We work with organisations of all sizes — from growing small businesses that need a dependable IT partner to mid-market and enterprise teams that need specialist consulting, project delivery or extra capacity.

What's the difference between IT consulting and managed IT services?

IT consulting helps you decide what to do — strategy, architecture, roadmaps and major projects. Managed IT services take care of running it day to day — monitoring, support, security and maintenance — for a predictable monthly fee. Many clients use both.

How quickly do you respond to support requests?

Response times are defined in your service agreement and prioritised by business impact, with critical issues handled around the clock. Most requests are resolved remotely, with engineers on-site when needed.

Can you work alongside our in-house IT team?

Absolutely. We regularly co-manage IT with internal teams — covering after-hours support, specialist skills like security or cloud, or extra capacity for projects — while you keep full control.

Do we own everything you build for us?

Yes. All code, configurations, documentation and credentials belong to you. We document everything so you're never locked in.
